6. Procurement Workflow

Departments submit a Procurement Request via Power Apps or InfoPath.
SharePoint stores the request in Procurement List.
Workflow steps:

Sends to Department Head for approval
If approved → goes to Procurement Team
Generates purchase order documents (library templates)


Procurement uploads PO, quotations, and billing documents.
Status updates to “Completed” after delivery.

2. Patient Admission Process

Reception opens the Patient Admission Form (Power Apps).
Fills in patient details → form submits to SharePoint Patient List.
A Power Automate flow triggers:

Notifies doctors or assigned ward
Updates “Admission Status”
Saves patient documents in Patient Library


Doctors review the admission record through SharePoint dashboards.

 Doctor Scheduling Process

Admin/Doctors update availability via a Power Apps scheduling form.
Data is stored in Doctor Schedule List.
Departments can view real‑time schedules through:

Calendar views
Filtered list views
Dashboards


Power Automate sends reminders or change notifications.
