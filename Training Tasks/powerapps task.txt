Mock CRM Project
Business Scenario
Goal: Manage Sales → Project Delivery → Billing → Support 

Overview
This custom CRM system is designed to support an IT services / consulting organization in managing its complete business lifecycle.
The system handles everything from initial customer enquiry through sales, project delivery, billing, and post‑delivery support, using custom Dataverse entities only.
